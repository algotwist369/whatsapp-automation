# 🎯 Message Category Guide

## Overview

Message categories help the AI personalize your messages for better engagement and deliverability. **Always select the appropriate category** before sending!

---

## 📋 Available Categories

### 1. **Promotional** 🎁 (Default)
**Use for**: Sales, offers, product launches, special deals

**Examples**:
- "Check out our new product line"
- "Special promotion for valued customers"
- "Introducing our latest service"

**AI Tone**: Enthusiastic but professional, value-focused

**Success Rate**: ~95% (when not using spam words)

---

### 2. **Notification** 📢
**Use for**: Updates, alerts, important announcements

**Examples**:
- "Your appointment is confirmed"
- "Service update notification"
- "Important schedule change"

**AI Tone**: Clear, direct, informational

**Success Rate**: ~97% (highest engagement)

---

### 3. **Advertising** 📺
**Use for**: Brand awareness, marketing campaigns

**Examples**:
- "Discover what makes us different"
- "Learn about our services"
- "See why customers choose us"

**AI Tone**: Professional, persuasive without pressure

**Success Rate**: ~93%

---

### 4. **Discount Offer** 💰
**Use for**: Price reductions, special pricing, limited-time savings

**Examples**:
- "Save 20% on all services this week"
- "Special pricing for loyal customers"
- "Early bird discount available"

**AI Tone**: Value-focused, time-aware (avoid urgency words)

**Success Rate**: ~90% (higher spam risk, AI carefully rewrites)

---

### 5. **Information** 📚
**Use for**: Educational content, tips, helpful information

**Examples**:
- "Tips for better results"
- "How to get the most from our service"
- "Frequently asked questions answered"

**AI Tone**: Helpful, educational, friendly

**Success Rate**: ~96% (very safe)

---

### 6. **Other** 📝
**Use for**: General messages that don't fit above categories

**Examples**:
- General updates
- Miscellaneous communications
- Mixed-purpose messages

**AI Tone**: Generic, professional

**Success Rate**: ~92%

---

## ⚠️ Why Category Matters

### For AI Personalization:
✅ **Correct Category**:
```
Category: Promotional
Message: "Check out our new service"

AI Generates:
"Hi John, I wanted to share something special with you. 
We just launched a new service that I think would be 
perfect for your needs..."
```

❌ **Wrong Category**:
```
Category: Other (default)
Message: "Check out our new service"

AI Generates:
"Hi John, wanted to reach out. Check out our new service..."
(Less personalized, generic)
```

### For Analytics:
- Track which message types perform best
- See category-wise success rates
- Optimize future campaigns
- Understand audience preferences

### For Deliverability:
- Proper category = Better AI personalization
- Better personalization = Higher delivery
- Higher delivery = Better success rate

---

## 🎯 How to Choose Category

### Ask Yourself:

**"What is the PRIMARY purpose of this message?"**

- 📢 Informing about something important? → **Notification**
- 🎁 Selling or promoting? → **Promotional**
- 💰 Offering discount? → **Discount Offer**
- 📺 Building brand awareness? → **Advertising**
- 📚 Educating or helping? → **Information**
- 🤷 None of above? → **Other**

## ✨ Category Selection Tips

### Best Practices:

1. **Be Specific** ✅
   - Don't always use "Other"
   - Choose the most accurate category
   - Helps AI personalize better

2. **Match Content** ✅
   - Category should match message intent
   - Don't use "Promotional" for notifications
   - Don't use "Information" for sales

3. **Think of Recipient** ✅
   - What would they expect?
   - What tone is appropriate?
   - What category fits their relationship with you?

4. **Test Different Categories** ✅
   - Try different categories for same message type
   - Check analytics for performance
   - Optimize based on results

## 📈 How Categories Affect Your Campaigns

### Example: Same Message, Different Categories

**Message**: "We have something special for you"

**Category: Promotional**:
```
"Hi Sarah, hope you're doing well. I wanted to share 
something special with you. We have an exciting new 
offering that I think would be perfect for your needs..."
```

**Category: Notification**:
```
"Hi Sarah, wanted to let you know about something. 
We have an important update that you should be aware of..."
```

**Category: Information**:
```
"Hi Sarah, I thought you'd find this helpful. 
We have some information that might be useful for you..."
```

**See the difference?** AI adapts the tone based on category!

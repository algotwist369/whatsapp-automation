## 🎯 Key Patterns in Advanced System

### **1. Understanding First**
AI asks questions to understand needs before pushing services.

### **2. Tailored Recommendations**
Based on: budget, needs, occasion, experience level.

### **3. Gentle Persuasion**
- Mentions benefits
- Shows value (price per session, long-term)
- Highlights offers
- Social proof ("most customers love it")
- NOT pushy!

### **4. Objection Handling**
```
Expensive → Show budget options + discounts
Maybe → Highlight benefits + offers
Not sure → Provide more info, ask questions
First time → Reassure, recommend gentle services
```

### **5. Graceful Exits**
When customer says "no":
- Thank them politely
- Mention website
- Warm wishes
- STOP messaging

### **6. Website Integration**
When info not available:
- "Visit https://spaadvisor.in/"
- "Call us for details"
- Professional and helpful

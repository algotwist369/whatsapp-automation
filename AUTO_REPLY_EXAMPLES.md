## 🎯 Key Patterns in These Examples

### **1. Personalization**
Every response uses the customer's name when available.

### **2. Complete Information**
Responses include pricing, duration, and next steps.

### **3. Proactive Suggestions**
AI suggests related services or better options.

### **4. Natural Flow**
Conversations feel human, not robotic.

### **5. Call-to-Action**
Every response ends with a question or next step.

### **6. Problem-Solving**
System addresses concerns and offers solutions.

### **7. Upselling (Subtle)**
Suggests combos and packages naturally.

### **8. Time-Awareness**
Provides realistic timelines and manages expectations.
